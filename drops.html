Reading a card

What the numbers on a drop actually mean

The ladder Four tiers

Green stops have unlocked. The red stop is the next price and the number of members standing between you and it.

Resale index 0.29 – 0.84

Median secondhand price over the last 90 days divided by original retail, pulled from public resale listings. A 0.84 means the pant holds most of its value; a 0.29 means the mugs do not. We publish it even when it argues against buying.

Origin line Named, always

Mill or assembly city on every card. A drop with no named maker does not run — that's Bylaw 3, and it's why the board is eight items and not eight thousand.

The clock Hard close

When it hits zero we capture the lowest tier unlocked and the factory gets the number. No extensions — an extended deadline is just a fake one.
