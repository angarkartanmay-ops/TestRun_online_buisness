======================================================================
1. WHAT IT IS
======================================================================
QUORUM is a group-buying website. A host starts a "drop" with a price
ladder. Every person who joins pushes the price down for everyone. The
price only falls, never rises. The cost receipt is published.

Example ladder (hoodie): 15 people = Rs 1,049 | 50 = Rs 899 | 100 = Rs 799.
Everyone pays the final unlocked price, whenever they joined. If the first
step is not reached, nothing is made and nobody pays.

Origin: you asked for "an online business that is Gen-Z native" with a
website. It became a buyers'-union site, then was rebuilt India-first.

2. WHAT WAS BUILT AND IS LIVE
======================================================================
- Stack: Next.js 16.4.3 on Vercel, Supabase Postgres, TypeScript,
  Tailwind, Drizzle ORM. 66 automated tests pass; build is clean.
- India-first: prices in Rs, IST dates, UPI payments, sizes, GST-inclusive
  prices, country of origin, returns/grievance/terms/privacy pages
  (drafts - need CA/lawyer review).
- Pay-after-close: joining is a promise. When a drop clears, members get a
  UPI link/QR or Razorpay payment link for the final price (48h window).
  No money is held, so nothing to refund if a drop fails.
- Two drop kinds:
    house   - QUORUM makes the product; margin guard blocks any tier under
              20% margin after GST, gateway and club fund.
    partner - an outside brand makes and sells; QUORUM takes a fee.
- Practice drops keep the board full (labelled, nobody pays).
- Admin: pilot-gate numbers, per-drop funnel, size order sheet, mark-paid,
  close payments, refresh practice drops.
- Fixed along the way: DB connection host, Next.js security advisories,
  Rs glyph in share cards, contrast failures, dead homepage, and 3 bugs
  (payouts never recorded, practice drops tripping overdue alert, late UPI
  payers losing their spot).

3. ASSESSMENT (NEED + SIMULATIONS)
======================================================================
Need in India: real but narrow. Campus/club/fest merch is the one clear
fit (today it runs on WhatsApp + Google Form + a printer, with someone
fronting cash and a 50-piece minimum).

Simulation results (informed estimates, not measured data):
- Ratchet ladder 15/50/100 clears 62% of drops vs 15% for a flat
  50-piece minimum (about 4x more club orders become real).
- Old 15/30/60 spacing lost money on 26% of cleared drops; fixed to
  15/50/100 aligned with factory price breaks.
- Marketplace at 8% fee nets about 2.6% of sales (thin, ~Rs 600/drop).
  15% fee: ~Rs 2,200/drop. Making the merch yourself: ~Rs 2,600/drop.
- 24-month outlook, full-time: median ~Rs 13k/month, 68% chance of
  profit, 11% chance of Rs 1L+/month. Part-time: roughly break-even.
- Two numbers decide everything: share of people reached who buy, and
  unit cost. Only real pilots can measure them.

VERDICT: Yes, as a cheap, time-boxed campus-merch experiment. Not yet a
full-time bet, a fundraise or an ad budget. Cash at risk before the
decision gate: about Rs 0-5k (samples).

5. HOW THE PRICE DROP AND EARNINGS WORK (latest question)
======================================================================
Price drop: ladder steps; price at join is only a ceiling; unlocked step
stays unlocked; everyone pays the highest step reached at close; below
the first step nobody pays. Sellers accept it because bigger batches cut
the factory price and each step sits where that price drops.

Will Adidas/Max sell here? Not now, probably not soon (they already sell
everywhere, protect pricing, produce in big seasonal batches, and QUORUM
has no audience yet). This is inference, not researched fact.
Realistic order of sellers:
  1. QUORUM's own label (campus hoodies/tees)
  2. Small Indian D2C brands wanting pre-orders without stock
  3. Creators / fandom brands (partner drops)
  4. Licensed brands, once there is an audience
  5. Big brands, only after thousands of active members, as special runs

Earnings:
- House drop example (tee Rs 549 x 50 = Rs 27,450): GST -1,307; gateway
  -648; club fund 5% -1,373; making+delivery -18,250; QUORUM keeps about
  Rs 5,900 (~Rs 118/tee).
- Partner drop (Rs 50,000 sold): 8% fee = Rs 4,000; minus 3% host share
  and gateway leaves ~Rs 1,300. Recommend raising to ~15% for outside
  vendors (one-line setting; NOT changed yet).
- Money flow: members pay after close -> pay factory (usually 50% advance,
  only from collected money) -> club fund paid by UPI after delivery ->
  remainder is QUORUM's earnings.
