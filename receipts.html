The $35 was never craftsmanship

A guaranteed pre-paid order removes four costs that retail exists to absorb. It does not remove one cent of material or labour — the mill, the sewing floor and the dye house are paid identically either way.

The honest part

Eight points on a $34 basket is not a comfortable business. Two things fix it and neither is raising the fee on members: category exclusivity deals at 11%, where a brand runs only with us for a season, and our own Local 001 drops — designed in-house, made at the same mills, sold at member price with a 34% gross margin because there is no third party to pay. Members pay the same either way.
