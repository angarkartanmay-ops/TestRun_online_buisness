How a drop clears

Four steps, and one of them is just waiting

Group buying isn't new. Doing it without hiding the margin is.

A host opens the drop

Anyone with a group can host: a server, a class chat, a run club, a creator with 80,000 followers. The host sets the item with the brand and posts one link.

Your card is authorised, not charged

Joining puts a hold on the opening tier — never a charge. If the drop misses quorum, the hold is released and you're told inside 24 hours.

Every new member moves the price down

Cross a tier and everyone drops to the new price, retroactively. Joining first costs you nothing — that's the part most group buying gets backwards.

The factory makes exactly the number ordered

We capture at the lowest tier unlocked, the brand runs the order plus a 3% defect buffer, and it ships in the window named on the drop. No warehouse, no markdown bin.

Bylaws

Six promises we'd have to break to make more money

Every one of these is a thing a normal marketplace does that we've given up. They're written here so you can hold us to them.

Bylaw 1 The price only falls

If members join after you, the difference returns to your card. We will never bill you the difference upward.

Bylaw 2 No quorum, no drop

Below the first tier nothing is manufactured and nobody is charged. Scarcity here is arithmetic, not a marketing tactic.

Bylaw 3 Every drop names its factory

Mill, city, country, on the card. Nothing is dropshipped. If a brand won't name the maker, the drop doesn't run.

Bylaw 4 8%, shown as 8%

Our fee is a line on your receipt. No markup buried in shipping, no "handling", no currency spread.

Bylaw 5 Membership is free, forever

No tier, no subscription, no paywalled early access. We earn only when a drop clears.

Bylaw 6 Made to the number

Production equals orders plus a 3% defect buffer. Returns go into the next drop at member price instead of a landfill.

Questions

The ones that actually get asked

What happens if the drop misses quorum?

The authorisation on your card is released and you're notified within 24 hours. Nothing is made, nothing is charged, and the drop either re-opens with a longer window or the brand walks. About 1 in 9 drops misses quorum; we publish that rate.

When am I actually charged?

On joining we authorise the current tier. When the window closes we capture the lowest tier that unlocked. If you joined at $41 and the drop closed at $23, we capture $23. You never have to ask for the difference.

Why does it take so long to ship?

Because nothing exists when you order it. Production windows are printed on every drop — usually four to seven weeks. That wait is the thing you're trading for the price. If you need it Thursday, this is the wrong shop.

Is this just wholesale with extra steps?

Wholesale needs a buyer with a credit line and a warehouse. We replace that buyer with a few hundred strangers and a deadline, which is why there's no minimum order, no unsold stock, and no markup paying for either.

Do you read my group chat?

No. There's no integration, no bot, no permissions request. A host gets a link with a code on the end; we count the code. Your chat stays a chat.

Can I return something?

30 days, any reason. Returns in sellable condition go into the next drop of that item at member price, which is also why the return fee is $4 rather than free — it covers inspection and re-bagging, and it's printed on the receipt like everything else.
