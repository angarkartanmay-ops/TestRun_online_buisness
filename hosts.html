The sequence

Four things to do, once

There is no content calendar and no posting quota. A drop is one link and one deadline.

Tell us what your group would actually buy

Not a category — an item. "My server would buy a heavyweight blank tee at $25" is a brief we can take to three mills by Friday.

We pair you with a brand and agree the ladder

You see the cost sheet before anyone else does. If the tiers look unreachable for your group, say so — you know your people better than we do.

Post one link

No affiliate disclosure gymnastics: the card says you're the host and shows your 3% as a line on the receipt. Your group sees exactly what you make.

Get paid on volume, not clicks

If the drop misses quorum you're paid nothing and so are we. Aligned by construction, not by contract.

Pairing

Brands and hosts get matched by the item, not the follower count

We keep a standing brief list from hosts — what their groups have said out loud they'd buy — and take it to makers. When a mill can hold a price at 300 units, we already know which four groups want it. That list is the only proprietary thing here, and it was written by members.
